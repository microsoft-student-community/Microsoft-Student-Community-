Your privacy.

This website is the public event guide for SYNORA at SRM University-AP.

What this website collects

This website has no participant accounts, registration forms, analytics trackers, advertising cookies, or payment fields. It does not store participant or card information in browser storage.

Registration and payments

Registration links open the organizer-provided Easebuzz form. The external provider and event organizers handle the information you submit there. Their privacy and payment terms apply. Check those terms before registering.

Public team listings

The public team grid includes only organizer-approved, opt-in team names, tracks, and registration statuses. Personal contact information is not displayed. Contact the event organizer through the registration page to request corrections or removal.

Hosting

The hosting provider may process ordinary access logs, such as IP addresses and request information, to serve and secure the website. Fonts, graphics, and interface resources are served with this site.

Questions

Use the organizer or support details on the official registration page for event, payment, and data requests.
